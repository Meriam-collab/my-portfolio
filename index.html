My favourite hobby: Hiking

I am pretty sure we all have our favourite hobbies, one way or the other. Well, my favourite hobby is hiking. I have explored quite a few hiking trails in and around Cape Town. I just love nature and being out there in the bush watching the waterfalls, hearing the birds singing and hugging my favourite trees. I know 'hugging trees' sounds weird but hey, it is very refreshing for me.

Why I love hiking

  • Reduced stress, anxiety, and depression. Nature exposure combined with physical activity significantly improves mood and mental well-being.
  • Weight management. Hiking can burn 400–550 calories per hour and supports greater weight loss than indoor exercise.
  • Cardiovascular improvement. Hiking strengthens the heart, improves circulation, and enhances VO2 max, reducing risks of heart disease.

List of hiking trails that I have explored

  1. Lion's head
  2. Devil's peak
  3. Pipe track
  4. Platterkloof gorge
  5. Silvermine nature reserve

Conclusion

I must say, ever since I started hiking I have been so energetic, calm and happy. I would like to encourage everyone to pursue hiking as their hobby. Whether for mental health, fitness or just relaxation. The health benefits are endless. What are you waiting for! Wear boots, invite friends or join hiking groups and get those boots muddy!
